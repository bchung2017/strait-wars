STRAIT WARS

A frigate underway under siege. Steer to dodge naval mines while your turrets hold off swarming drones, missiles, and gun-boats.

◀ ▶ buttons (or A / D / arrow keys) steer the ship left, center, right to dodge naval mines
CRAM turrets fire automatically — multiple rapid-fire guns lead and shred the nearest threats
Laser turrets fire automatically — they pick and burn the nearest threat
MISSILE button (or 1) launches an interceptor at the nearest incoming missile
DRONE button (or 2) deploys an autonomous anti-drone drone
Survive the crossing — travel the distance on the left NAV gauge to clear each wave. Every wave is a longer leg.
Your defenses are autonomous — you command the missiles and drones. RESET restarts anytime.

Tap or click to begin. Landscape recommended on mobile.
